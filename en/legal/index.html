Legal notice and privacy

Who publishes this site, where it is hosted and how the information you send us is handled.

Publisher

Espérance's Hope association (Côte d'Ivoire), working against hydrocephalus and spina bifida.
Publication manager: Espérance ALIA BROALET, President.
Address: Neurosurgery department, CHU de Yopougon (Côte d'Ivoire).
Email: esperancehope.assoc@yahoo.com

Hosting

The site is hosted by GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc., 88 Colin P. Kelly Jr. Street, San Francisco, CA 94107, United States).

Information you send us

If you write to the association by email, your address and message are used only to reply to you. They are neither sold nor used for any other purpose, and are kept as long as needed to reply.

Please do not include medical details by email. This site is not an emergency service.

You may ask to see, correct or delete your information by writing to esperancehope.assoc@yahoo.com.

Cookies and statistics

This site uses no tracking cookies and no analytics tool. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts servers, which can see the visitor's IP address at that moment.

Photos and video

The images and video shown belong to the association. Anyone who wants an image or clip of themselves removed can write to us and we will remove it.
