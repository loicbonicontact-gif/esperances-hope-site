November 2026 campaign

Screening and free consultations campaign

Agneby-Tiassa region, 2 to 8 November 2026. “Act early to prevent disability”.

World Spina Bifida and Hydrocephalus Day

For World Spina Bifida and Hydrocephalus Day, the Espérance's Hope association is running an awareness, screening and free neurosurgical consultation campaign in the Agneby-Tiassa region. This is the seventh edition of these activities.

The programme: informing communities about spina bifida and hydrocephalus, screening children with neurological abnormalities or malformations, offering free consultations to children and adults (low back pain, cervical arthrosis, headaches…) and training health staff in early diagnosis.

2 November

Taabo hospital (EPHD)

Activities started at 9 a.m. at the Taabo hospital (EPHD). When the delegation arrived, everything was already in place: a tent for the awareness session on spina bifida and hydrocephalus, three offices for free consultations with three doctors, a waiting hall, and a fourth office freed up in the afternoon for a fourth doctor.

The association's coordinator led the awareness session under the tent, together with the delegation's nurse.

The delegation included 4 doctors (2 neurosurgeons, including the association's President, and 2 neurosurgeons in training), 1 nurse and the coordinator.
